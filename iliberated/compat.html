The compatibility list is coming soon.

We don’t have a published, fork-specific game list yet. iLibirated targets 32-bit apps for iPhone OS 2.x, iPhone OS 3.x, and iOS 4.x, with a focus on games. Support is incomplete; not every app for those versions will run.

Before you play

A few things to know

  • The native app targets iPhone and iPad running iOS 15 or later.
  • Use legally obtained .ipa files or .app bundles. The app binary must be unencrypted.
  • 64-bit iOS apps are outside the scope of this emulator.

Help the project

Your results make a difference

When reporting a game, include its name and version, your device and iOS version, the iLibirated version, and what happened when you tried to play.

Compatibility can differ from iDared and touchHLE. Please send results to this fork’s issue tracker or support email.
